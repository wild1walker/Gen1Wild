# Wild Green

**This is, I think, the definitive way to play Red.**

Every quality-of-life and visual upgrade in the Gen1Wild suite, in one cart,
and only the ones that still feel like Red. That was the whole filter.
Sprinting, autosave, a Pokédex worth opening, a bag with pockets, battle
backdrops, animated Crystal sprites, a Pokémon walking behind you: none of it
turns this into a different game. It is the game you remember, with the parts
that made you put it down taken out.

**And you can catch every single one of them.** All 151, in one save, on one
version, without trading. The legendaries are retryable until you land them,
the trade evolutions are handled by an item, and Mew waits behind the Mansion
journals where it always should have been. Every encounter the cartridge
already had still behaves exactly as it always did.

So if you are going for a 100% Kanto dex, or you just want Red to feel like a
game made this decade, Wild Green is the most enjoyable way to do it.

## It plays as its own version

Wild Green is a **custom cart**: a named, fixed set of mods that plays as its
own game, with its own entry in the launcher, its own cartridge and its own
save slots. Nothing here writes into your base Red saves. Two people running
Wild Green are running the same game.

It pins four things: **Gen1WildQOL** and **Gen1WildUI** (the two halves of the
suite), **Crystal Animated Sprites with Shiny Visuals** (somebody else's mod,
pinned as it is), and **Wild Green** itself, which puts the player in green and
`WILD GREEN VERSION` on the title screen. The green is derived from Red's own
art rather than shipped, so switching back to `PLAYER = RED` gives you the
original character with nothing to reinstall.

The mod set is sealed, so you cannot add to it, but every feature inside it is
still a switch you can flip. A cart you could not play your own way would miss
the point.

## Installing it

Install from this card, or grab the `.g1rcart` from
[the cart's releases](https://github.com/wild1walker/Gen1WildGreen/releases)
and open it from **Custom Carts > Import a cart**. If a pinned mod is missing,
the cart's own page offers **Install required mods** and fetches them for you.

No game data, no ROM bytes. You bring your own, exactly as the engine already
asks.
