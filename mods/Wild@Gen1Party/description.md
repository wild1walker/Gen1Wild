# Gen1Party

The party menu, drawn like the rest of the set.

## Six Pokémon, six sets of colours

Vanilla lays one palette over the whole icon column, so every Pokémon in your
party comes out wearing the same salmon. Here each one wears its own species
colours, the same thing Gen1Dex does for its list and Gen1BillsBox does for
its grid. Open your party next to either of them and it stops looking like a
different game.

The names, numbers and boxes go back to reading as black line art, the way
every other screen in the set does. And if a menu-icon mod has drawn its own
full-colour art, that art is left exactly as its author drew it, right down
to a shiny telling itself apart from an ordinary one of its kind.

## Nothing runs off the edge any more

The vanilla row is packed to the very last pixel of the screen, which is
authentic and is also why a status like `FNT` reads as clipped rather than
placed. Everything now stops at the same right margin every other screen in
the set keeps, and nothing was given up for it. The HP bar keeps all six
segments, the numbers keep their padding, no name got shorter.

## Switches

**SPECIES COLOURS** off restores the vanilla answer exactly, for anyone who
wants the 1996 screen with nothing changed but the margins.

## Worth knowing

This mod has an opinion about how the party *looks* and none at all about what
it does. The seven different jobs that screen does are untouched: the field
menu, the battle switch, the item target, the TM/HM list, all of it.
