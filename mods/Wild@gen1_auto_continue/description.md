# Gen1AutoContinue

Press START at the title screen and your save loads. That is it. No CONTINUE /
NEW GAME menu, no PLAYER / BADGES / POKéDEX window to acknowledge. One press
instead of four.

Boot goes straight to the title too, past the copyright card and the attract
movie. Title, one press, playing.

The title screen itself keeps everything worth keeping: the logo drop, the
cycling Pokémon, the cry, the white-out. That part is the game announcing
itself and it costs you nothing. The menu you answer the same way every single
time is what goes.

## The buttons

**START or A** loads your save. **B** exits the game. **SELECT** gives you the
ordinary menu back, in full, any time you want it, so nothing is actually
taken away.

B and SELECT do nothing at all on the vanilla title screen, so neither is
borrowed from anything.

## Worth knowing

If there is nothing to continue, the main menu appears as it should. First
boot, a deleted save, a save too damaged to load: all of them land where you
would expect.

Everything is switchable: the skip, the B exit and the intro skip are three
separate options. Link play is unaffected, other title-screen mods still work,
and on Gold, which has no main menu to skip, the mod simply sits out.
