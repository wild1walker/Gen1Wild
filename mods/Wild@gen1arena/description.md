# Gen1Arena

Battles happen somewhere. A wild encounter in Viridian Forest gets trees
behind it, a gym trainer gets the gym, a Goldeen you hooked on Route 12 gets
open sea, and Lance gets Lance's own scene. The white void is gone.

Nothing else about a battle changes. It is a picture behind the sprites, drawn
at the game's own resolution in both the classic and widescreen layouts, with
nothing resampled.

## It knows where you are

The backdrop follows two things: where you are standing, and how the battle
started. Grass on a route, the route itself for a trainer, caves, forests,
towns in their own colours, open sea or a lake or underwater depending on what
you are swimming in, and one scene each for Giovanni, the Elite Four and the
Champion.

The towns are worth a word. The eleven Kanto cities differ in exactly one way
in the GBC overworld: the roofs change colour. So that is what the town
backdrops do, using the game's own roof palettes rather than an invented tint.
Cerulean is blue, Fuchsia is magenta, Cinnabar is red. Gyms follow their town.
Interiors are left alone, because a Pokémon Center looks like a Pokémon Center
everywhere in the real game too.

## Worth knowing

Built for the **ADVANCED** colour mode, since the other modes put full-colour
art behind four-shade sprites. And because Gen 1 back sprites use the white
field for their light shades, your own Pokémon can read as a silhouette
against the darker scenes.

Switch it off entirely with one option if you would rather have the white.

**None of the art was drawn for this mod.** Every backdrop comes from the
**Battle Backgrounds Patch FR** for Pokémon FireRed, and its authors ask for
credit wherever it is used:

> **LibertyTwins · princess-phoenix · carchagui · aveontrainer · WesleyFG ·
> kWharever · worldslayer608 · knizz**

What the mod adds is the picking, a colour correction into the GBC range, a
crop to the two layouts and the per-town roof recolours. The composition,
linework and colour in every scene are theirs. Carry those names anywhere the
art goes.

Parts of that pack are original fan art and parts are FireRed-derived, and the
files do not distinguish between them, so unlike the rest of this index, this
one is **not** MIT and ships no blanket licence. The code is Wild's; the art is
credited, not relicensed.
