# Gen1MenuManager

Make the START menu yours. Reorder the rows, hide the ones you never touch,
and pin the field items and moves you use constantly so they get a row of
their own.

The Pokémon Center PC menu works the same way, and each keeps its own
arrangement. Open the editor from the START menu and you are editing the
START menu; open it from a PC and you are editing that.

## What you can pin

The TOWN MAP and the BICYCLE, once they are in your bag. Each rod, once you
have it. FLY, CUT, SURF, STRENGTH and FLASH, once a party member knows one and
you hold the badge for it. DIG and TELEPORT as soon as somebody knows them.

A pin only appears once you have actually unlocked it, so the menu grows with
your playthrough rather than starting out full of rows that do nothing.

## Using it

Press **SELECT** with either menu open, or pick the **MENU MGR** row, or find
it on the OPTION screen. Three ways in, and no arrangement you can make closes
all of them.

**A** grabs a row, up and down move it, **A** again drops it. **SELECT**
shows or hides a row, or switches a pin on and off. **B** leaves.

## It plays well with everything else

Other mods' rows are arrangeable too. QUESTS, ACHIEVEMENTS, anything else
somebody appends can be moved and hidden like a vanilla row. A row that turns
up later is added rather than dropped. And the PC's LOG OFF can never be
reordered or hidden, so there is always a way out.

Your layouts follow your save, and a new file starts from your last
arrangement rather than from scratch.

## Worth knowing

Everything above is a switch: the SELECT shortcut, either MENU MGR row, and
whether a pin hides itself when it cannot be used right now, like the BICYCLE
indoors or SURF facing dry land.

Link play is unaffected, and it works on Gold as well as Red, Blue and Yellow.
Requests **no permissions**, and contains no ROM-derived content.
