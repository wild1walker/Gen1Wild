# Gen1WildQOL

Half of the Gen1Wild suite in one mod: everything that makes Red *play*
better, and nothing that makes it a different game.

Hold a button and run. Autosave, so a bad guess never costs you an hour. Boot
straight into your save instead of answering the same menu every time. The
low-HP siren beeps once instead of forever. A Pokémon walking behind you. All
151 catchable in a single file, without trading. Party-wide EXP. A move your
Pokémon forgot, handed back. Menus you can rearrange, HMs without the menu
dance, and a banner naming the area you just walked into.

Thirteen features, from ten different places, in one install.

## Take as much or as little as you like

Every feature is its own row in `OPTION > GEN1WILD QOL`, on or off, with its
own settings one press of `A` behind it. Want the sprint and the autosave and
nothing else? That is two switches, not a different download. Nothing here is
all-or-nothing, and nothing here is on that you cannot turn off.

`MENU LAYOUT` and `MOD MANAGER` are also in
[Gen1WildUI](https://github.com/wild1walker/Gen1WildUI). Install both bundles
and they sort it out between themselves, with no collisions and no duplicated
work.

## Before you install

Uninstall the standalone versions of anything above. They install the same
hooks, so the manifest declares them as conflicts. Settings do not carry over.
